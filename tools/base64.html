Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode plain text into Base64 or decode Base64 back into readable text.

What is Base64?

Base64 is a method of representing binary data as text. It is commonly used when transferring data between systems.

Note: Base64 is an encoding format, not encryption. Never use it to protect passwords or confidential information.
